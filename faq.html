Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I apply for a job?

Browse available listings on the Jobs page, click "View Details", and click "Apply Now" to submit your details.

Q: Can I apply for more than one job?

Yes, you can apply for multiple part-time job opportunities that fit your academic schedule.

Q: How can I check my application?

Navigate to the "My Applications" tab in the navigation bar to view your status updates.

Q: What if I forget my password?

Contact system support through the Contact Us page or request a password reset from the admin team.

Q: Is there any payment required?

No, using the Student Part-Time Job Portal is 100% free for all registered university students.
